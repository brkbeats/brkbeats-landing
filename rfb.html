On the MPC

A front panel on the touch screen.

🎚

Ten Faders That Follow the Mode

In FB they set the ten bands. In FILTER, CLK MOD and DYN EQ they take that mode's controls, each named on the screen, so the same ten faders play every mode.

🔘

A Button Over Every Band

Hold one and its band swings in time. In the other modes it locks its band. With SHIFT, the buttons are the feedback taps that send bands back into the resonance.

📶

LED Ladders for Every Band

A ladder for each band, left and right, showing its level as it moves: swinging, stepping, or cut by DYN EQ.

🎛

All on the Q-Links

Sixteen Q-Links: the four knobs, the ten faders, VOLUME and SWING. They stay on the panel even with the plugin's screen closed.

A second page, SETUP, shows the ten frequencies of the current set and holds what the panel has no room for. The MPC version sounds the same as the computer's: every factory preset renders identically on both. You activate it from the desktop app with the MPC connected by USB, and the plugin can sit on any drive. Tested on an MPC One G2 with MPC 3.10.1.

What it does

Four ways to play the bank.

FB

Set Every Band by Hand

Boost and cut ten bands, then feed bands back into the resonance, all the way to self-oscillation.

FILTER

Shape the Whole Bank

Turn the ten bands into a low-pass, high-pass, band-pass or band-reject curve, with its own cutoff, slope and width.

CLK MOD

Step Through the Bands

A clocked modulator walks the bands in any direction, at its own BPM or locked to the track's tempo.

DYN EQ

Bands That React

Bands that get loud are cut as you play. Turn on BOOST and the quiet ones are lifted too.

Also Included

Made to move.

〰️

Hold a Band to Make It Swing

Hold a band's button and it swings up and down in time. SWING sets how far, SWING RATE how fast, from two bars to a sixteenth. LATCH keeps as many swinging as you like.

🎼

Retune the Whole Bank

Four frequency sets: the original ten bands, a major scale, the harmonic series and a blues scale, with ROOT to move them.

🔁

Stereo Modulation in Time

On the computer, every band, RESONANCE, MIX, SPREAD and each mode's controls can have their own LFO and envelope follower, left and right apart, free or locked to the tempo.

💡

32 Factory Presets

From LOW GROWL and SUB PULSE to DICE ROLL and SNAP, the same on the computer and the MPC, and listed in Logic's own preset menu.

↔️

Left, Right or Both

One switch sends the faders and feedback taps to the left channel, the right, or both, for stereo that moves apart.

📊

See What It's Doing

On the computer, a live display switches between the bands, the spectrum and the modulation as you work.
