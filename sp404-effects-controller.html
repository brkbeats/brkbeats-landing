FAQ

How does it connect?

What do I need?

An SP-404MKII and one USB cable that carries data. Some cables only charge. Nothing else: no MIDI interface, no audio interface, no 1/4" cables.

Do I need a DAW?

No. The standalone app controls the SP-404's effects directly, on whatever the 404 is playing: pads, its own sequencer, DOOM OS, or a source plugged into its input. Open the app and it finds the SP-404 by itself.

Do I have to set up MIDI?

No. The app finds your SP-404 over USB and talks to it directly. There's no MIDI track, no external instrument and no channel setup.

How does it work in a DAW?

The SP-404 also works as a USB audio interface, so a track can go out to it, through its effects, and back over the same cable:

  1. Put SP-404MK2 Effects on an aux bus, as an audio effect.
  2. Add your DAW's external-hardware insert to the same bus, set to the SP-404. In Logic that's the I/O plugin; in Ableton, External Audio Effect.
  3. Send any track to that bus, and turn input monitoring on.

The plugin passes audio through untouched, so what you hear is the SP-404's own effects. Every parameter can be automated in your DAW.

Can I use it on more than one track?

Use one instance, on one aux bus, and send whichever tracks you want to it. The SP-404 is a single piece of hardware, so several copies of the plugin would fight over it.

I can't find the plugin in my DAW.

Look under audio effects, not instruments. It's filed under the maker name brkbeats, which some DAWs sort to the bottom of the list.

Does it work with DOOM OS?

Yes. It's tested with DOOM OS 0.6-alpha, and it works on the stock firmware too.

iPad or iPhone?

Not yet. Mac and Windows only for now.
