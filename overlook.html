Why I built it

The tool I kept wishing existed.

I love my Tonverk, and I kept wanting the same things around it: slicing loops into kits without a trip through a DAW, bringing my sampled instruments across whole, and moving a pattern from one project to another, effects and all.

So I built Overlook. It works with the files on your Tonverk's card — kits, instruments, wavetables, and projects — and everything it writes is something the Tonverk loads like its own. It runs on the Mac or the PC you already have.

What it does

Everything the Tonverk loads,
from your computer.

🥁

Drum Sets

Slice a loop on a grid, at its hits, or by hand, and the pattern plays it back with its feel. Or build kits from one-shots, with velocity layers and round robins. A melody or a vocal loop follows your tempo in its own key.

🎹

Instruments

Map a folder of samples to keys, every one measured and played in tune. Line up attacks, trim the silence off tails, and find sustain loops so held notes keep sounding. On a Mac, Auto-sample plays your synth over MIDI and maps what comes back.

📦

Instrument Imports

SFZ, Decent Sampler, Logic and GarageBand Sampler, MPC programs, SoundFonts, and Ableton Live presets, racks, sets, and Live Packs, without Live installed. Drum kits come in as drum sets, their pads tuned as they played. Batch-convert a whole folder.

🌊

Wavetables

Generate one, take one from a sample, or stack a folder of single cycles into a table. Serum, Vital, CarveToy, and MiniFreak V tables open at their own size, and you choose which 64 waves go to the card. Always saved with the _wt name the Tonverk reads the size from.

🎼

Tracks

A step sequencer with velocity, length, micro timing, swing, retrigs, trig conditions, and PROB. Chords, arp, and glide as on the machine. Up to 128 patterns in a project. MIDI files in and out, and record what you play on the Tonverk's keys or any keyboard.

🎲

Vary, Keep and Rotate

Turn one pattern into many: Vary moves trigs to other pads and changes slices, the rhythm and the downbeat staying. Keep the trigs you like, Rotate the rest one slice on, and A/B two patterns against each other.

📚

Sort by Type, the Slice Library

Kicks on one pad, snares on the next, and the pattern moves with them. Every slice from your named pads goes into a library by type: fill a pad with 16 kicks at random, or pick the ones you want.

🗂️

Projects and Songs

Your drum sets and instruments on the eight tracks, their patterns, and up to 16 named songs with row mutes. Copy any pattern on the card into another project, its kits, samples and knobs with it. ▶ starts the Tonverk too.

⏏️

Onto the Tonverk

Send a kit to the card, or put the kit and its pattern on any of the eight tracks of a new project or one already there, ready to load. Open any kit on the card, factory ones too: tested on a full card, every kit the Tonverk plays read back identical.

🎛️

MPC Projects

Send a project to the Akai MPC: each pattern a sequence at its own length, your songs as MPC songs, and every pad its own track, with its own mixer strip and effects. Instruments go as keygroup programs. Tested on an MPC One G2 with MPC 3.10.1.

🎹

Tonverk Instruments on the MPC

Open a Tonverk .elmulti, free packs included, and it goes to the MPC as a keygroup program, every sample as it is. A WAV chopped on the MPC opens in Overlook already cut.

🎼

MIDI Files for the Tonverk

One file per pattern, each track on its own channel and each slice as the velocity that picks it, so a DAW plays the Tonverk as its own sequencer does.

💾

Back Up with Samples

A project on the card and every sample it uses, in one folder on your computer, to keep or send. Restore puts it back on a card, writing over nothing.

📁

Collect Samples

Every sample a project uses, copied into the project's own folder on the card, so that one folder is the whole project.

🔢

More Than 128 Slices

A pad holds every slice of a long loop. Random no-repeat deals from all of them, and a send takes the 128 the pattern plays. Pads you mute arrive muted on the Tonverk.

Safety first

Your projects come first.

💾

Backed Up First

Anything that writes into a project backs the whole project up to your computer before it touches it.

✋

Asks Before Replacing

Nothing on the card is overwritten without asking — and it names every project that uses a kit before you replace it.

⏏️

Safe to Eject

Everything is flushed to the card, and the app tells you when it's actually safe to eject.

What you need

The computer you already have.

Runs on the computer you already have: any Mac from 2015 on (macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Intel or Apple silicon), and Windows 10 or 11.

An Elektron Tonverk and its SD card is what Overlook is built for; the sound-making tools work without one. MPC projects are tested on an MPC One G2 with MPC 3.10.1. An internet connection is needed once, to activate.
