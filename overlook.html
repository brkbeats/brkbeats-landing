Why I built it

The tool I kept wishing existed.

I love my Tonverk, and I kept wanting the same things around it: slicing loops into kits without a trip through a DAW, bringing my sampled instruments across whole, and moving a pattern from one project to another, effects and all.

So I built Overlook. It works with the files on your Tonverk's card — kits, instruments, wavetables, and projects — and everything it writes is something the Tonverk loads like its own. It runs on the Mac or the PC you already have.

What it does

Everything the Tonverk loads,
from your computer.

🥁

Drum Sets

Slice a loop on a grid, at its hits, or by hand, and the pattern plays it back with its feel. Or build kits from one-shots, with velocity layers and round robins. The preview plays the kit the way Subtracks does.

🎹

Instruments

Map a folder of samples to keys, every one measured and played in tune. Line up attacks, trim the silence off tails, and find sustain loops so held notes keep sounding. On a Mac, Auto-sample plays your synth over MIDI and maps what comes back.

📦

Instrument Imports

SFZ, Decent Sampler, Logic and GarageBand Sampler, MPC programs, SoundFonts, and Ableton Live presets, racks, sets, and Live Packs, without Live installed. Drum kits come in as drum sets, their pads tuned as they played. Batch-convert a whole folder.

🌊

Wavetables

Generate one, take one from a sample, or stack a folder of single cycles into a table. Serum, Vital, and CarveToy tables open at their own size, and you choose which 64 waves go to the card. Always saved with the _wt name the Tonverk reads the size from.

🎼

Tracks

A step sequencer with velocity, length, micro timing, swing, retrigs, trig conditions, and PROB. Chords, arp, and glide as on the machine. Up to 128 patterns in a document. MIDI files in and out, and record what you play on the Tonverk's keys or any keyboard.

⏏️

Onto the Tonverk

Send a kit to the card, or put the kit and its pattern on any of the eight tracks of a new project or one already there: drums on one track, an instrument on another, ready to load.

New in 0.9.1

From one pattern
to a whole set.

A free update for everyone who bought 0.9.

🎲

Vary…

Turn one pattern into several. Trigs move to other pads on the same steps, slices change, a trig or two gets a PROB, and the rhythm and the downbeat stay. Each copy goes a little further than the last, and one undo takes them all back.

🎯

PROB

Give a trig a chance of playing, as on the TRIG page. The preview and the Set roll it, and the Tonverk gets it as its own PROB.

🅰️

Duplicate and A/B

Duplicate a pattern to change it, A/B to hear the two against each other, and name them Verse and Chorus.

📂

Any Pattern, Any Project

Hear the drums and samples of any pattern on the card without loading it on the machine, then copy it into another project. Its kits, samples, chords, scales, trig conditions, and knobs come with it.

🗂️

Set

Build patterns from your documents, one per track, and hear them together: every pattern in turn, or in your own song order. Send set puts them on the card as one new project. The song order stays in Overlook for now.

▶️

Starts the Tonverk

Press play in Overlook and the Tonverk starts with it, over USB. Stop stops both.

A closer look

One app for every kind
of file the Tonverk loads.

Works with your card

Opens what's already there.

OPEN

Your kits, factory ones too

Open any .eldrum or .elmulti on the card to see and hear every region. Tested on a full card: 265 of 266 kits read back identical, field for field.

SEND

Straight to the card

Send a kit to the card, or make a new project with it on a track and the pattern already in it. One click, then Safe to eject. Every kit sent remembers which document it came from, and opens it again.

PLAY

Ready to load

Wavetables go in with the name the Wavefinder needs, instruments with every sample on its key, drum sets with their patterns. Load them on the Tonverk and they play as they did in Overlook.

Safety first

Your projects come first.

💾

Backed Up First

Anything that writes into a project backs the whole project up to your computer before it touches it.

✋

Asks Before Replacing

Nothing on the card is overwritten without asking — and it names every project that uses a kit before you replace it.

✅

Checked Before Writing

Projects are checked before every write, and a damaged one is never written to. A kit whose sample can't be found isn't written either: you're told which file, and where it looked.

⏏️

Safe to Eject

Everything is flushed to the card, and the app tells you when it's actually safe to eject.

💻

A Copy on Your Computer

Each time the card is mounted, Overlook copies its projects to your computer, only reading the card. Look through them with the card back in the Tonverk.

🎧

Documents Keep Their Sounds

A saved document keeps a copy of the audio it uses beside it, so it still opens after the files you started from have moved.

What you need

The computer you already have.

Runs on the computer you already have: any Mac from 2015 on (macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Intel or Apple silicon), and Windows 10 or 11.

An Elektron Tonverk and its SD card is what Overlook is built for; the sound-making tools work without one. An internet connection is needed once, to activate.
