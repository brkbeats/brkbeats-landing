Polyphonic Synthesizer

A synth that shows you what it's doing.

Ottava is an 8-voice analog-style synth with a built-in scope, so you can see every change you hear. Learn synthesis one knob at a time, roll random sounds by type, and render your own one-shots and sample packs in a click.

What it does

See it, hear it, keep it.

📈

A Scope That Teaches

Five views: each oscillator on its own, the finished sound, the filter's frequencies with the cutoff marked, and the envelope with a dot following every note. Turn a knob and watch what changes.

💡

82 Presets That Point the Way

Bass, lead, pad, keys, pluck, FX, drone and a full drum kit, all set to the same loudness. Every preset names the knob to try first, and the Tour walks through the synth one control at a time.

🎲

Random Sounds, by Type

Click RANDOM, or the scope, for a new patch. Choose a type — kick, snare, hat, bass, pad, FX and more — and every roll stays in that lane. Undo puts the last sound back.

💾

Render One-Shots and Sample Packs

One click saves the current sound as a WAV, trimmed and normalised. Or render 10, 20 or 50 random kicks at once, each with its preset beside it. 24-bit at 44.1, 48 or 96 kHz.

🥁

A Drum Kit Built In

Kicks, snare, clap, rim shot, open and closed hats, tambourine, shaker, tom, conga, cowbell, woodblock and crash. Every one is a clean one-shot, tuned and ready to play on C2.

❓

Tips on Every Control

Rest the pointer on any knob for a line on what it does and what it will sound like. Switch the tips off once you know your way around.

🎛

Analog-Style Sound

Two band-limited oscillators with six waveforms, a sub and four colours of noise. A diode low-pass plus four more filter types, a tunable comb filter, two LFOs, and stereo unison.

🎹

Plays Like an Instrument

Eight voices, velocity, sustain pedal, mod-wheel vibrato and pitch bend. Unison plays legato and glides between overlapping notes. Right-click any control to assign a MIDI knob.

Tuning

Built-in microtonal tuning.

SCALE

19-EDO

19 equal steps to the octave, for lush thirds and xenharmonic textures beyond standard 12-tone tuning.

SCALE

22-EDO

22 equal steps to the octave — a brighter, stranger flavor of xenharmonic tuning, ready out of the box.

SCALE

Just Intonation

Pure-ratio tuning: chords in C ring without beating. Made for drones, pads and anything that leans on natural harmonics.

The tuning stays where you put it, so you can play every preset in 19-EDO, 22-EDO or Just Intonation. Four presets are built on these tunings to get you started, and the guide shows the chord shapes for each.

Your sounds

The sounds you make are yours.

Anything you record or render with Ottava belongs to you, royalty-free — including sounds made from the factory presets and the randomiser. Use them in your own music, or sell and share samples, one-shots and sample packs made with it. No credit required. The only thing this doesn't cover is Ottava itself: the plugin and its factory preset files.
